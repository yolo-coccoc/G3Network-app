## How to use this log

- **IDs are stable**: `<AREA>-<NN>`. A new decision takes the next number in
  its area. A decision is never rewritten to mean something else: a change of
  mind is a **new entry** that supersedes the old one, and the old one moves to
  its area's *Superseded* table.
- **Status**: ✅ confirmed · ⏳ interim / open (a working choice until decided) ·
  📦 deferred (recorded in `deferred.md`, item number given).
- **Source** points to where the detail lives (planner section and its own
  decision ID such as `D12`, rules file, or DBML note). Planners keep their
  local `D1..Dn` tables; this log is the cross-project index of them.
- Questions still waiting for an answer are listed under
  [Open questions](#open-questions) below (technical and vendor questions)
  and in the DBML `open_decisions` (data-model business questions;

## Open questions

Not decided yet. For each one, keep the interim proposal and build
accordingly (PR-07); an answer becomes a decision entry above or below and
the question is removed here. Numbers are stable ("open question 4").

1. **CI/CD**: which platform to use (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins...)? Not yet decided. Interim decision (2026-10-01): local pre-commit hook (`make check`) only; remote is GitHub, so GitHub Actions is the natural candidate when CI is set up.
2. **Secrets management for production** (Vault, AWS/GCP Secrets Manager, or just `.env` + CI secrets)? Not yet decided.
3. **Local connection protocol** between the Telematics device and the vehicle screen (BLE/Wi-Fi Direct/CAN...) — out of current scope since `vehicle-app/` has no source yet; must be finalized before starting to code the `core/` module of the vehicle app.
4. **Willdigits charger — information required from the vendor** (OCPP 1.6J integration). Not yet answered; interim proposal: build against the OCPP 1.6J standard and the real logs (raw message log + post-boot `GetConfiguration`), assuming no vendor-specific behaviour. Blocking requests: (a) OCPP Implementation Guide / Interface Document; (b) mapping of the 80 internal error codes to `vendorErrorCode` (CSV/JSON); (c) DC meter brand/model/accuracy class/tamper seal; (d) confirmation of `wss://` and the authentication mechanism (Basic Auth or client certificate). Other: written list of supported OCPP profiles; multi-level HMI permissions; whether VIN Autocharge runs over OCPP; offline buffer capacity; whether `DataTransfer` is used. Details: `docs/design/specifications/charging-station-specification-summary.md` §6.2. Added 2026-10-05 for the QR flow (CO-14): what the QR codes on the charger's screen contain (Charger ID and gun?); whether, after a `RemoteStartTransaction` without a connector, the driver chooses the gun and presses Start on the screen; how `AuthorizeRemoteTxRequests` is set.
5. **Non-software blockers for the charger project**: (a) truck inlet standard — CCS2 or GB/T for Tri-Ring EVT-262/400/825; (b) DC meter verification in Vietnam (decides whether kWh invoicing is legal, or a service-fee model is needed); (c) warranty and technical-support provider in Vietnam. Interim proposal: nothing in code; the backend stores meter readings as reported and does not claim they are legally verified.
7. **Erasing a person's personal data on request** (Decree 13/2023, Law 91/2025/QH15): a soft delete (DM-25) keeps every row, but a data subject may ask for their data to be erased. Not decided: which fields are wiped or anonymised (name, phone, e-mail, licence, positions linked to them), what must be kept for legal or billing reasons and for how long, and how history tables and logs are treated. Interim proposal: nothing in the design yet; settle with the legal adviser before launch.
8. **Driving time across organizations**: the legal limits (4 h continuous, 10 h a day, 48 h a week) apply to the person, but each driving session belongs to the truck's organization (DR-07, DR-11), so a driver who drives trucks of two organizations on one day can exceed them while each organization's report stays within them. The sessions lead to the same user, so the system can add them up; open is who may see the total. Options: (1) only the driver, in the DRV-05 warning; (2) also the organization checking the driver in, as hours left only, never where or for whom; (3) also refuse a check-in at the limit. Questions for the legal adviser: may the organization checking a driver in see their hours driven elsewhere, and is it responsible for them? Interim proposal: nothing is shown across organizations; each organization's reports count the sessions on its own trucks; decide before DRV-05 is built.

9. **Trucks without our T-Box** (e.g. an organization with conventional diesel trucks that wants our fleet management): the QR check-in works for any registered truck, but everything built on T-Box data does not (location check at check-in and auto-end of driving sessions, DR-07; the alert for a truck moving with nobody checked in; live map, trips, reports, geofences), and `telemetry.soc_percent` is required, so a diesel truck's data would not fit. Options: (1) no device: only the non-data features, check-in without a location check; (2) install our T-Box: GPS, ignition and movement, with telemetry's battery fields made optional; (3) read the tracker the truck already has (the journey-monitoring device commercial trucks must carry under Decree 10/2020) from its provider's API, one integration per provider. Interim proposal: nothing designed; owner postponed it on 2026-10-10.

10. **Battery warranty terms behind the charging policies** (policy domain, POL-01 to POL-06): the `policy` tables cannot be designed until the warranty conditions are known, because each kind of rule is a column with its own checker in code (rule kinds are code, values are data). Questions for Tri-Ring / the battery supplier: (a) which charging or usage conditions void or reduce the battery warranty (battery % limits, fast-charge share, temperature, deep discharges, charging hours, power); (b) is each measured per charge or over time (e.g. a weekly share, a battery-health trend); (c) what a violation leads to (a warning, a penalty, a void warranty); (d) who assigns a policy (our warranty team per truck, per fleet, per model, per customer contract). Interim proposal: the policy group review is parked (owner, 2026-10-10); the four planned tables stay as first sketched, and the generic shape discussed (versioned rule values, assignments to a truck, fleet or model with the most specific winning, violations pointing to the exact version) is the starting point once the answers arrive.

11. **Legal boundaries of the prepaid wallet** (PAY-07): only banks and licensed payment intermediaries may issue e-money (e-wallets, prepaid cards; Decree 52/2024), while a balance usable only for one's own service is normally a prepayment for services (V-GREEN's prepaid service account works this way). To confirm with a legal adviser before launch: (a) whether an organization may move balance to its drivers' wallets (method 2) and whether transfers between people are allowed at all; (b) whether and how unused balance may be withdrawn or refunded; (c) limits on balances or top-ups and consumer-protection duties; (d) public naming ("ví" in the app, never "ví điện tử"); (e) accounting: a top-up is an advance (a liability), and the e-invoice is issued for the charging service, not the top-up. Interim proposal: `wallets` hold balance for our own services only; no transfers between people and no cash withdrawal at launch; refunds of unused balance only through the original payment channel.

Answered and removed: 6 (ID-43). The next new question is number 12.
